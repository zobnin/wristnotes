# Wrist Note Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 22, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how **Wrist Note** (`org.execbit.rpker`) handles information.

## Summary

Wrist Note does not collect or transmit personal data to the developer. The app has no Internet permission, uses no developer-operated servers, and contains no advertising, analytics, tracking, or crash-reporting services.

Wrist Note processes the text of your note locally on your Android device. When you choose **Send to watch**, the app places that text inside a watch application and shares the resulting file with Gadgetbridge so that Gadgetbridge can install it on your connected wearable.

## Information handled by the app

Wrist Note handles the following information only on your devices:

- **Note content.** Text that you enter or paste is saved automatically as a draft in Wrist Note's private app storage.
- **Generated watch application.** Your note is included in a temporary watch application file stored in Wrist Note's private cache. The file also contains non-personal build metadata, such as its creation time and general Android CPU architecture.
- **Local version number.** A counter is stored in private app storage so that each newly generated watch application has a newer version.

Wrist Note does not request or access your identity, email address, phone number, contacts, location, health information, photos, media, microphone, camera, advertising ID, or financial information.

You are not required to enter personal information into a note. If you choose to include personal or sensitive information in its text, that information is handled locally and transferred only as described below.

## Transfer to Gadgetbridge and your wearable

Wrist Note transfers information only when you tap **Send to watch** and confirm the installation in Gadgetbridge.

The generated watch application contains the full text of your note. Wrist Note grants the separately installed Gadgetbridge app temporary read access to that file. Gadgetbridge may then transfer it to the wearable selected in Gadgetbridge. Wrist Note does not communicate with the wearable directly and does not receive data from it.

Gadgetbridge and the wearable are separate products outside Wrist Note's control. Their handling of the transferred file and any other information is governed by their own software, settings, and policies.

## Data collection, sharing, and sale

The developer does not receive, collect, store, sell, or use your note or any other personal data.

Wrist Note does not share information with advertisers, analytics providers, data brokers, or other online services. The user-initiated transfer of the generated watch application to Gadgetbridge is performed solely to provide the app's main feature.

## Permissions and security

Wrist Note does not request Internet, Bluetooth, location, storage, contacts, camera, or microphone permissions.

Draft text and generated files are kept in Android private app storage. Android backup is disabled for Wrist Note. Its file provider is not publicly accessible and grants read access only to the generated watch application when you initiate installation through Gadgetbridge.

No method of local storage or transfer can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Keep your Android device, Gadgetbridge, and wearable protected and up to date, particularly if your note contains sensitive information.

## Retention and deletion

- The draft remains in private app storage until you edit or erase it, clear Wrist Note's app data in Android settings, or uninstall Wrist Note.
- The latest generated watch application remains in Wrist Note's cache until it is replaced, Android clears the cache, you clear the app's storage, or you uninstall Wrist Note.
- The note installed on your wearable remains there until you replace it with another note, uninstall Wrist Note from the wearable through Gadgetbridge or the wearable's app manager, or reset the wearable.

Because the developer never receives this information, the developer cannot view, recover, or delete it remotely. Wrist Note does not provide accounts, so there is no account data to delete.

## Children's privacy

Wrist Note does not knowingly collect personal data from children or anyone else. All information handling is local and initiated by the user as described in this policy.

## Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated if Wrist Note's features or data practices change. The effective date at the top of this page will be updated when a new version is published.

## Contact

For privacy questions or requests, use the developer contact email shown on Wrist Note's Google Play store listing and include **Wrist Note privacy** in the subject line.
